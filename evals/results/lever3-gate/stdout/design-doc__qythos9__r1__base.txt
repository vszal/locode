The reaper runs every 1 second, so a crashed worker will have at most 5 seconds of lost progress before recovery.\n\n**No orphaned claims**: Because the claim timeout is enforced server-side (in SQLite), even if a worker process is killed mid-claim, the database state reflects that the claim has expired.
