Claims expire after a certain period to prevent deadlocks.\n\n## Trade-offs Rejected and Reasons\n\n- **Distributed System**: While a distributed system could provide higher availability, it introduces complexity and additional dependencies. If a handler raises an exception, it is considered a transient failure and will be retried.
